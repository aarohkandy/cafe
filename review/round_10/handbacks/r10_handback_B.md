## M-6. One tolerance for the coverage gate and the chance figure

Changed (`whatif.py`, then moved in the rework below): `COVERAGE_DUST = 1e-9`; `_coverage_in(cov, nominal)` = `abs(cov - nominal) <= LIMITS["coverage"] + COVERAGE_DUST` (now `coverage_band(nominal, LIMITS["coverage"])` from `emulator.py`, same set of counts); `_chance_miss(n_test, nominal)` (the binomial probability of a hold-out count outside it) built on `_coverage_in`. `fit_class` takes `coverage_ok` and `coverage_chance_fail` from these two. Before, the gate had no tolerance and the chance figure had `+ 1e-12` on the other side of the comparison.

Checked: `emulator.receipts()` compared a quotient `k / n` with the literals 0.85 and 0.95 and was exact at the edges, so it needed no change for the dust. It was still a second copy of the limits (see "Rework" below), which is now removed.

Callers checked: `fit_class` is the only user of either rule. `pipeline.s_emulator` prints `info["coverage_chance_fail"]` and `whatif.LIMITS["coverage"]` unchanged; `report._chance` only prints the number; `test_a_missed_coverage_gate_alone_completes_the_run...` sets `LIMITS["coverage"] = 0.0` and still gets chance 1.000 (a hold-out that is not exactly nominal misses by more than 1e-9).

Effect on the figures (exact, Fractions; K ~ Binomial(n_test, 0.9)): the probability that a calibrated emulator fails the gate is now the printed one.

## M-1. The emulator is trained and queried per scope (class, platform, window length)

Lead decision followed: the scope form. A variant never changes the window or the platform, so a feature would be constant and degenerate the GP and the z-scoring; a scope refuses to pool what is not the same function. No feature was added (BASE/TASK, the feature counts, the KPLS switch at d > 10 and the range guard are unchanged).

What a scope is: `budget.emulator_scope(mission, vid, cls)` = `"<class>/<platform>/<window hours>h"`, e.g. `glider/seaglider/144h`, `usv/saildrone_explorer/144h`, `glider/slocum/36h`. The window is `window.end - window.start` (what a variant cannot change), not end minus deploy: the deploy offset is already the feature `deploy_h`. It lives in `budget.py` because `budget` is below `whatif` and the plan has to count by it. A vehicle the mission does not list gets platform `?` (hand-made plans in tests).

Every reader of the table, traced:
- Writers: `whatif.vehicle_rows` adds the `scope` column; `row_key` includes it (so the same mission name with a different window or platform is not "a rerun" and does not replace the rows of the other scope); `add_rows`, `with_rows`, `snapshot` concatenate frames and need nothing (an older table gets NaN in the new column).
- `_clean(df, cls, scope)` keeps only rows of the class and the scope. `key_cols` is unchanged (the point identity inside one scope). `fit_class(df, cls, scope, path, ...)` counts `MIN_ROWS` points of the scope only, and its info gains `scope`, `other_scope_rows`, `unscoped_rows`. The refusal says what the scope holds and what it does not pool: `"60 glider/seaglider/96h rows in the training table, 100 are needed (a 20 % hold-out must score 90 % coverage); 110 glider rows of other platforms or window lengths are not pooled with them, since P(fail) over another window or platform is another function"`, plus `"; N glider rows made before rows carried a scope are left out"`.
