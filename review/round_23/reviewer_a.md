### A-2. The Iridium note printed beside every Iridium verdict says a member "cannot miss the required fraction by chance"; for `required_fraction` 1.0 (the schema's maximum) about 1 member in 10 does
severity: minor
lane: honesty / statistics
where: cafe/pm/report.py:1827-1832 (`IRIDIUM_NOTE`, printed by `_iridium_html` for any link whose method starts with "Iridium"); cafe/schemas/mission.json:67 (`required_fraction` exclusiveMinimum 0, maximum 1); cafe/pm/physics/comms.py:322-338 (`iridium_session`); docs/PHYSICS.md 12.3 ("with 865 independent samples a member cannot miss 5 % of them by chance" is the 0.95 case only)
reproduction: the per-sample failure of an SBD message of 340 bytes is `(1 - p_success) ** max_attempts` = 0.05**3 = 1.25e-4 (one session, three attempts; measured 2 failures in 20,000 draws of `comms.iridium_session(340, rng, 20000)` on orca). A mission with an Iridium link `when: always` and `required_fraction: 1.0` over the demo's 865 moments passes a member only if no moment misses: P(member misses at least one of 865) = 1 - (1 - 1.25e-4)**865 = 0.1025 (computed in `probe2.py`). So about 10 % of members fail by chance alone, the link's P(success) is about 0.90, and the card beside that verdict still says that a member needing hundreds of samples "cannot miss the required fraction by chance". The sentence is conditioned on the number of samples and not on the required fraction; it is true for 0.95 (the demo) and false for 1.0 and for 0.9999.
why it matters: the sentence is printed as the reason the verdict is no evidence, and it is the sentence a lab user reads to decide whether an Iridium NO-GO or MARGINAL at `required_fraction: 1.0` is chance (it is the assumed 0.95 and three attempts, but it is chance, not "cannot").
wrong data: yes (a printed explanation beside a verdict that is false for `required_fraction` near 1; the verdict itself restates assumed numbers, as the note otherwise says)
