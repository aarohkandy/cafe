How it works

cafe answers one question before a field deployment: will this fleet of underwater, surface and aerial vehicles do its job together, in the weather that day actually had?

  1. Describe each vehicle

    A few dimensions or a CAD file, plus mass, power and battery.

  2. Characterize it once

    OpenFOAM for drag and hull lift, Capytaine for added mass, CalculiX for collapse depth and loads.

  3. Fly the plan

    Every vehicle in real WRF wind, HYCOM currents and WAVEWATCH III seas, many times with the uncertain inputs varied.

  4. Read the verdict

    Go, marginal or no-go for every vehicle and every range requirement, with the reason.

The chart and the full simulator

The chart page runs a quick check in your browser: each vehicle moves at the speed its CFD-derived power curve allows, is carried by the real current or wind, and spends its battery accordingly, under the same failure rules and verdict thresholds as the full simulator.
