## 5. A zero-filled wave file passed every ingest check as "a calm"
- Fix: a sampled sea point is calm only when the wind there is calm too: `CALM_WIND_MS = 0.2` (a fully developed sea 0.21 U^2 / g is CALM_SWH_M high at 0.22 m/s) and `_sample_wind` (the larger of WRF's 10 m wind at the node and hour and the wave file's own `wspd`, NaN when neither is known, which is not calm), used by both consumers: `_spectrum_errors` counts `calm` only for swh <= CALM_SWH_M under that wind and counts the rest in the new key `flat`; `_ustar_agreement` counts a point whose UMWM ust is at or below USTAR_FLOOR_MS under a wind that is not calm as a point with an infinite relative difference (so it is compared and disagrees, not skipped). The three spectrum checks and the u* check then fail, and the spectrum notes say "N of M sampled sea points have swh at or below 0.001 m under a wind above 0.2 m/s (WRF, or the wave file's wspd): the wave file is zero or was never written, which is no calm". The signature of `_ustar_agreement` is the same (4 values; its first value now counts the compared points including those).
- DECISIONS row text: A calm sea of the ingest checks (M-6) is a sea with no waves and no wind: a sampled point is calm when swh <= 0.001 m and the larger of WRF's 10 m wind there and the wave file's own `wspd` is at most `CALM_WIND_MS` = 0.2 m/s (a fully developed sea of 0.21 U^2 / g is 1 mm high at 0.22 m/s). Zero waves under a wind are a wave file that is zero or was never written: the three spectrum checks fail naming it, and a UMWM ust at or below `USTAR_FLOOR_MS` under a wind counts in the u* agreement as an infinite relative difference, so the u* check fails (median infinite) instead of passing as "not assessable". A real calm (everything zero, wind included) still passes with no value. Measured on the real demo cache (q11, orca, 2 CPUs): the u* agreement is 888 sampled sea points, median 0.0398, 98.76 % within 20 % and the spectrum errors (906 points, none flat) are the numbers in q11's ingest ledger, so no printed number of the demo moves. Tests: `tests/test_pipeline.py::test_l0_r11_zero_waves_under_a_wind_are_a_broken_file_not_a_calm`; the two M-6 tests now zero the wind as well (P-R11L0-2).
- Amends: the M-6 row (a calm is decided from the wind too).
- CHANGELOG: A zero-filled UMWM wave file under a wind fails the ingest checks as a broken file; only a sea with no wind either passes as a calm (round 11, look-ahead L0).
- Could break / checked: the readers of the two functions are `_ingest_checks` and tests (the 4-tuple is unchanged; tests that monkeypatch `_ustar_agreement` return 4 values and still work); credibility reads the u* fraction from the ledger value, which is unchanged for real data (measured above). A partly zero file now moves the u* median and fraction (those points count as disagreeing), which is the right direction; no real file in the data directory has such points (0 of 888). Known edge: a UMWM run that starts from rest has swh ~ 0 at its first hour under a wind; the demo cache has none (hour 0: minimum wind 4.2 m/s, swh above the floor everywhere sampled), but a cache with a cold-start hour in the sample would fail the spectrum checks only if every sampled point of the whole sample is flat (the mixed case is judged on the points with a swell, as before).
