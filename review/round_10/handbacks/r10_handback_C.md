## M-2  `nest.running_mean_ok` judges the mean (BATCH-MEANS)

Change: `nest.running_mean_ok` takes the last third, splits it into `RUNNING_MEAN_BLOCKS` = 5 batch means (`np.array_split`, the same construction and count as `windows.blocks` / `coefficients` nb = 5; it cannot import `windows.blocks` because `windows` imports `nest`), and takes the running mean of those 5 values against the last-third mean. `(ok, dev)` shape and the 0.02 limit unchanged.
Test: `tests/test_nest.py::test_r10_m2_the_running_mean_judges_the_mean_not_one_sample`: white noise 5 % (n 3000, seeds 0 to 4) passes (largest dev 0.006); AR(1) rho 0.95 with 2 % marginal sd passes (largest 0.0145); a ramp of the last third from 1 to 1.06 (the first sample 3 % off the mean, the old figure) fails with dev 0.0233, and still fails with 5 % noise on it; `np.ones` gives `(True, 0.0)`.
Honest limit: AR(1) at rho 0.95 with a 5 % marginal sd does NOT pass any 2 % test of the mean over a third of 3000 samples (the standard error of that third's mean is about 1 %, its first fifth about 2.2 %); I measured dev 0.004 to 0.036 over seeds at 5 % marginal. The test uses 2 % marginal sd for AR(1) for that reason. If the owner wants the 5 % AR(1) to pass, the limit or the block count (a burn-in) is a different decision.
Sensitivity: the new test is less strict than the old one by design. A 5 % ramp over the last third (old dev 0.0244, fails) now gives 0.0195 and passes; a 6 % ramp gives 0.0233 and fails. The 2 % limit now means "the running mean of five batch means stays within 2 % of the final mean".
Callers checked: `windows.stage3_checks` (windows.py:217) is the only caller; the row `force_running_mean_dev` keeps its value/limit meaning; the text at `pipeline_windows.py:473` ("the force running mean moves X over the last third") stays true. No stored receipt, report text or credibility row reads the old definition (grep). Consequence: windows with ordinary force fluctuation now pass this row and can reach the refit, so any GPU run's refit and golden can change. The q10 run has no LBM rows, so it neither shows nor refutes this.
DECISIONS row: "`nest.running_mean_ok` (BRIEF 5.5 'force time-series converged: running mean within 2 % over the last third') takes the running mean of five batch means of the last third against the last-third mean, instead of the running mean of the samples. The limit 0.02 and the `(ok, dev)` shape are unchanged. | Review round 10, M-2. The sample running mean restarted at the first sample of the last third, so its deviation was at least that sample's own noise (5 % noise failed 4 seeds in 5 although the third's mean is known to 0.2 %), and 'no convergence' was untrue. Five batch means is what `windows.coefficients` already uses for its spread. A real drift of 3 % from the mean still fails. Not measured on real LBM drag." Amends: none (no earlier row covers it); reads BRIEF 5.5 as 'the mean has converged', not 'every sample is within 2 %'.
CHANGELOG: "Stage 3: `force_running_mean_dev` judges the running mean of five batch means of the last third, so a converged series with per-step noise above 2 % no longer fails as 'no convergence' (round 10, M-2)."
Doc sentences: PHYSICS.md:957 `force_running_mean_dev` (0.02): add "the running mean of five batch means of the last third against the last-third mean (a sample's own noise is not a convergence failure)". BRIEF.md 5.5 is the owner's and was not touched: the DECISIONS row states the reading.
