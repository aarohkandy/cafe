## Findings

### C-1. RUNBOOK tells the user to read a GO of a partly-needed link "as a GO on the members that needed it"; it is not one
severity: minor
lane: documentation, statistics, honesty
where: docs/RUNBOOK.md:188 (last clause of the NOT EXERCISED bullet); the numbers it contradicts are `stage1.summarize` (`cafe/pm/stage1.py:948`, `v = intervals.verdict(int(f.sum()), n)` over all n members) and the card sentence `report._never_required_html` (`cafe/pm/report.py:1679`)
reproduction: the bullet says that for a link some members never needed "in those members the pass fraction is 1.0 by default and they count as passes in the P(fail) beside it, so read a GO of such a link as a GO on the members that needed it". The first half is true (stage 1 gives such a member `frac` 1.0 and `success` True). The conclusion is not: with n = 64, 40 members that never needed the link and 3 failures among the 24 that did, the printed verdict is `intervals.verdict(3, 64)` = GO (P(fail) 0.047, upper 0.112), while the members that needed it are `intervals.verdict(3, 24)` = MARGINAL (0.125, upper 0.275). Likewise 6 failures: GO (0.094, upper 0.171) on 64 and MARGINAL (0.25, upper 0.415) on 24. Run: `python3 -c "from cafe.pm.stats import intervals as I; [print(k,n,I.verdict(k,n)['verdict']) for k,n in [(3,64),(3,24),(6,64),(6,24)]]"` in the worktree (pure numpy). The card (correctly) says only that those members count as passes; no page prints the P(fail) or verdict on the members that needed the link, so the RUNBOOK sentence is the only place that tells the user what to read, and it tells the wrong thing.
why it matters: a user who follows the RUNBOOK takes a GO that rests on 24 members' worth of evidence (and, here, a 12 % to 25 % failure rate among them) for a GO of the link in the conditions that needed it. This is the exact risk row 997 exists to warn about, stated backwards in the one document that says what to do.
wrong data: yes: the verdict GO of a link that only some members needed, as the RUNBOOK says to read it (not seen in q21: no link of the demo has `n_never_required` above 0).
