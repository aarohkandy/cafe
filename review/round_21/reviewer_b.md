### B-1. The cause of Bellhop's two-direction difference is tested now, and it is not what PHYSICS 832, `comms.py:475-478` and DECISIONS 1017 say; the 'biased low by half the spread' sizing is wrong in kind
severity: minor
lane: physics / documentation
where: `docs/PHYSICS.md:832`, `cafe/pm/physics/comms.py:475-478`, `DECISIONS.md` row 1017 ('the minimum of two noisy estimates ... biased low by about half their spread'), `cafe/pm/stage1.py:641-645`
reproduction: `rev21_b_p1.py`, `_p2.py`, `_p3.py` (arlpy Bellhop through a copy of `_run_bellhop`, 9.5 kHz and 18.5 kHz, range 2 to 7 km, 4,000 m water, Mackenzie profiles, `mode=incoherent`, `nbeams=0` unless stated).
- It is not a shadow and it is not beam count. On the 'sharp thermocline' profile (24 C to 12 C between 20 and 30 m) the mean TL over 2 to 7 km is 87.50 dB for (tx 1 m, rx 5 m) and 92.57 dB for (5 m, 1 m): 5.07 dB mean, 13.1 dB at worst. With `nbeams` 8000 it is 86.44 / 93.14; with Bellhop's coherent mode (nbeams 0 and 8000) it is 91.9 / 97.1 and 89.9 / 97.8. So the same asymmetry in two run types and two beam counts.
- It is a property of an end at 1 to 2 m depth. Same profile, mean TL forward minus reverse, by depth pair: (5, 25) +0.01 dB, (10, 50) -0.07, (30, 100) +0.12, (5, 6) -0.01, (15, 5) +0.01 (reciprocal to 0.1 dB); (2, 10) -1.72, (1, 15) -1.37, (1, 50) -1.23, (1, 5) -5.07, and (1, 2) +19.33 (75.73 against 56.40 dB, and 56 dB is below the 73 dB of spherical spreading alone at 4.5 km, so unphysical). `stage1._bellhop_member` clips every surfaced glider to exactly 1.0 m, so the one transducer depth every `when: surfacing` acoustic link has is the depth at which Bellhop breaks reciprocity.
- The error is one-sided, not noise. The deeper-sender direction is stable: the excess over the isovelocity column (the quantity `acoustic_link` adds) of the (5 m, 1 m) direction is 18.14, 17.57 and 18.29 dB for `nbeams` 0, 1000, 4000; the (1 m, 5 m) direction is 13.18, 9.92 and 8.63 dB. Deeper-pair references agree with the stable one: mean TL of the reciprocal pairs is 91.5 to 91.8 dB against 92.57 (the lower margin's direction) and 87.50 (the spuriously low-loss one). The lower of the two margins is therefore the converged answer to about 1 dB on this profile, not 'biased low by half the spread' (which was 2.5 dB mean here and 4.8 dB at `nbeams` 4000). Tropical and Munk-like profiles: spread 1 to 2 dB, same sign pattern.
why it matters: the rule (lower of two) is right and now has its reason; the three texts still say the cause is 'probably ... a shadow, not tested' and size the bias as a noise effect, and DECISIONS 1017's alternatives (b) the mean would be the worse choice (it averages in the spurious direction) and (c) 'the shallower transducer first' would be the worst (it is the broken direction). A lab reader choosing between the alternatives is told the wrong trade.
wrong data: no
