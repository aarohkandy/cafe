**M-1 (OD-1 default (a), the lead's notes).** The card of every RF link and L23 print, per pair of heights, the MEDIAN of the model's loss minus the Fresnel loss and the COUNTS of the 400 sampled ranges that are more than 3 dB overstated and more than 3 dB understated, and then one sentence that says these are figures of the sampled ranges and not the worst case. It gains the note and a clause on the deck height (M-4).
- `cafe/pm/report.py`: `_low_antenna_note` prints, for one pair, `for this pair of heights (x m with y m): <figures>. <note>; <tail>`, and for several pairs `by pair of heights: <pair> <figures>; ... . <note>; <tail>`. The card says plainly what the lead asked: "These are figures of the 400 sampled ranges, not of the worst case: at an interference null the model's error is larger than the median, and a finer sampling of the ranges can find a larger one". I wrote "can find", not "grows" or "usually finds": see section 2, the measurement says "grows" is false for about a third of the pairs.

**M-4.** `report._end_heights(end, mission, cls, when)`: a UAS end is at deck + level + antenna for each level (as before) AND, unless `when == "uas_airborne"`, at deck + antenna (on the deck; the card says "uas (on the deck at 8.1 m, flown at 18.1, ... m)"). `_low_antenna_note` passes `lk["when"]`. **M-12 (`rb_off`).** One test with samples at exactly +3.0 and -3.0 dB (and just beyond each): both counts are strict, in both signs.
