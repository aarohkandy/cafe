### M-14 (the P.526 smooth-earth diffraction loss is in the RF model)
`comms.smooth_earth_diffraction_db(d, ht, hr, f_hz, a_eff)`: beyond the radio horizon the ITU-R P.526 spherical-earth field -(F(X) + G(Y_t) + G(Y_r)) (vertical polarisation over sea of 72.8 / 6.05 S/m, k = 4/3); inside the horizon P.452's line-of-sight interpolation (the loss over the effective earth radius that grazes the path, times the clearance-weighted fraction (1 - h_se / h_req)), never negative. `two_ray_loss_db` in the transition zone (below `psi_critical`, up to the horizon) is now `max(curved, plane-earth, free-space(direct distance) + diffraction)`; above `psi_critical` and beyond the horizon nothing changes.
