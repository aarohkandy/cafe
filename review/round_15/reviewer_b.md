severity: major

reproduction: from `q15_mission.json`, `vehicles.uas.sorties[i].wind_ms` (the launch gate's mean wind at deck + 30 m) against `wp_done` (n_wp 8), 384 sorties. Share of sorties that reach 8 of 8 waypoints, by wind: 3-4 m/s 0.98 (n 62); 4-4.6 1.00 (47); 4.6-5 1.00 (38); 5-5.5 0.98 (56); 5.5-6 0.47 (45); 6-7 0.00 (66); 7-8 0.00 (27); 8-11 0.00 (16). The tilt clamp is active (`sat_s` median) 8 s at 3-4 m/s, 160 s at 4-4.6, 462 s at 4.6-5, 624 s at 5.5-6, 912 s at 8-11 of a 1200 s sortie; correlation of wind with `sat_s` 0.92. At member level the split is perfect: a member has `profile_incomplete` if and only if its largest sortie wind is at least about 5.8 m/s (the 9 members that never fail peak at 5.67 m/s or less, the 55 that fail peak at 5.90 or more). So "UAS NO-GO 55 of 64" is "some one of 6 sorties had a wind above 5.8 m/s", read off a threshold. Arithmetic: upwind the quad must hold 8 + w of airspeed, the platform's own limit is 12.6, so 8 + w > 12.6 from w = 4.6 m/s; the 20 min budget (`time_limit_factor` 1.0) then breaks once the headwind slows the leg (each 2.15 km leg takes 269 s at 8 m/s, four of them are 1075 s of the 1200 s before climbs and the landing).

A pilot (and stage 3) would fly the legs at an air speed the airframe can hold and stretch the sortie; stage 1 does neither, and the verdict measures the commanded ground speed, not the quad. Either command airspeed (or cap the ground speed by `v_max - w`), or scrub at the wind where the plan cannot be flown, or state in the report that the verdict is this threshold.

### B-2. glider_eq's MARGINAL verdict (18 of 64 lose the station) holds only for a 950 m dive that the stage-3 hull check says the vehicle cannot make; at the depth the hull survives it is NO-GO 64 of 64 (a consequence P-R14V4 does not name)
severity: major
lane: physics
where: missions/tepex_c_demo.yaml (`glider_eq` `dive_depth_m` 950); cafe/pm/physics/glider.py:_begin_dive (`target_depth` = min(task depth, `rating_m`), no hull limit from stage 3); cafe/pm/stage1.py:_run_glider

Why: the current the glider meets is the Equatorial Undercurrent: eastward, median 0.52 m/s (5 to 95 %: 0.29 to 0.89) at 100-200 m, 0.38 at 20-100 m, 0.22 at 200-400 m, but -0.03 at 400-700 m and -0.12 at 700-1000 m (the run's 1728 records x 64). A 950 m dive spends 59 % of its flying time below 400 m where the current is small and negative, a 364 m dive spends 95 % of it between 20 and 400 m: the mean eastward current along the flight is 0.10 m/s over the run at 950 m (5 to 95 % of members: 0.07 to 0.16) and 0.39 m/s at 364 m (0.25 to 0.53); the dive cycle falls from 7.9 h to 3.4 h but the station is lost by every member. In a still analytic ocean with a uniform current (`rev15_b_still.py`, 4 members, 6 days, tolerance 5 km, 12 h) the 950 m Seaglider keeps station at 0.1 m/s and loses it at 0.2 m/s, the 500 m one holds at 0.2 and loses it at 0.3: the loss is the depth-mean current against the 0.2 to 0.28 m/s swim speed, and the 18 of 64 is the lower tail of that.
why it matters: the stage-3 FEA collapses the default hull at about 364 m (stress use 0.6975, buckling factor 0.766) while the verdict, the cross-section the report draws and the 18 of 64 all assume 950 m. Nothing in the pipeline connects the two, so the glider_eq verdict is the answer to a question the hardware cannot be asked; with the hull's own depth the answer is the opposite class (NO-GO).

### B-3. `becalmed` ignores the direction of the current, so a cross-track current that cannot stop a northward glide is counted as "unable to stem it"
severity: minor
lane: physics
