## Item 2: Bellhop cost and grid

### (a) a sample beyond the cap is not refined (`stage1._acoustic`, label in `_bellhop_label`, `merge`)
- `far = need & valid & (|Thorp margin| > comms.BELLHOP_EXCESS_CAP_DB)`; `refine = need & valid & (|Thorp margin| <= cap)`. A far sample keeps its Thorp margin. A NaN margin (no wind there) is neither: no run, stays NaN (before: it was refined and came back NaN).
- Proof (`tests/test_round21_m.py::test_a_sample_beyond_the_cap_has_the_same_sign_refined_or_not_and_the_cap_is_as_tight_as_it_can_be`): the real `comms.acoustic_link` with a Thorp margin made exactly linear and a Bellhop excess of -1e6, -cap, -cap/2, 0, cap/2, cap, 1e6: every sample with |margin| > cap, at +-(cap + 1e-6) too, has the sign of its Thorp margin refined (pass is `margin > 0`); and the cap is as tight as it can be: at +cap, at cap - 1e-6 and at -cap + 1e-6 a refined sample CAN flip, at -cap, cap + 1e-6 and -cap - 1e-6 none does. The edge itself is pinned through `evaluate_link` (cap monkeypatched to the sample's own Thorp margin: refined; one ulp below: not), both signs.
- The label (`bellhop` info gains `beyond`; `merge` sums it): "Thorp/Stojanovic budget on the slant range, Bellhop on {done} of {total} needed in-range samples[ (the lowest of n runs: ...)][; k of them held at the assumed 60 dB bound ...]; a further {beyond} needed in-range samples are more than 60 dB from a pass or a fail on Thorp and keep that margin: the Bellhop excess is held within 60 dB, so it cannot change which they are" (the last clause only where beyond > 0; byte-equal to batch A's label where it is 0). Where every needed in-range sample is beyond: "Thorp/Stojanovic budget on the slant range; Bellhop was not run: all N needed in-range samples are more than 60 dB from a pass or a fail on Thorp, and the Bellhop excess is held within 60 dB, so it cannot change which they are". `total` is what Bellhop was to refine, so "the rest Thorp only (no sound-speed profile or depth there)" still means a member with no profile.
