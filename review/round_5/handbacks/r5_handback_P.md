### M-5 (UAS cost follows the sortie-minutes)
- `cafe/pm/budget.py`: `sortie_minutes(mission, classes)` (launches from `stage1._uas_setup`/`launch_times`, at least 1, times the sortie duration), `bench_sortie_rate(entry)` (the entry's `sortie_min` / `mission_days`, else computed from the mission it names, so the stored file needs no re-measure), `scale_uas_costs` (called in `plan`; scales the UAS vehicle's `s_per_batch_day` and `s_per_member_day`, in place, and appends "x19.8 for 2380 sortie-minutes in 6 days against the benchmark's 20 per day" to the cost's `source`).
